Features

OAuth 2.0 Login

Secure authentication via TikTok Login Kit with PKCE. Your credentials never leave TikTok's servers.

Video Upload

Upload MP4, MOV or WebM files directly to TikTok. Supports videos up to 4GB.

Privacy Controls

Choose who sees your video: public, friends only, or private. Full control over your content.

Cross-Platform

Works on any device with a modern browser. No installation required.

How It Works

Login

Click "Login with TikTok" and authorize Uploaderbot to access your profile and video list.

Select Video

Choose a video file from your computer. Add a title and set the privacy level.

Publish

Click publish and the video is uploaded to your TikTok account via the official API.

About

gravy-diaper-refrain is a web application that enables TikTok creators to publish videos directly from their desktop browser through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Why this app?

  • No app installation required
  • Works from any desktop browser
  • Direct integration with TikTok's official API
  • Your data stays private — we never store videos or credentials
  • Open source

Data Handling

Uploaderbot only accesses the data necessary to provide the service: your public profile info and video list for authentication, and video upload/publish capabilities to post content. Access tokens are kept in memory during your session and discarded on logout. No videos or personal data are stored on our servers.
