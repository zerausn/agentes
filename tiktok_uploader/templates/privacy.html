Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Information We Collect

When you authorize Uploaderbot via TikTok OAuth, we collect:

  • Basic profile info (username, display name, avatar)
  • Profile details (bio, profile link)
  • Public video list and metadata

We do not collect private messages, email addresses, or phone numbers.

How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data solely to provide video upload and publishing functionality. Your access token is used only for API requests you initiate and is never shared with third parties.

Data Storage

Access tokens are stored temporarily in server memory and are discarded after your session ends. We do not permanently store your TikTok data on our servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to third parties. Authorized API requests are made directly to TikTok's servers.

Your Rights

You can revoke Uploaderbot's access at any time through your TikTok account settings. You may also clear your session by logging out.

Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us through the TikTok Developer Portal.
